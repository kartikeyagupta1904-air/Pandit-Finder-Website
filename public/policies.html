Policies

These policies describe how this application behaves. Have them reviewed by a qualified professional before using the platform commercially.

Privacy policy

  • We collect your name, email, password (stored only as a secure bcrypt hash), and optionally your phone and city.
  • Pandit phone numbers, emails and identity documents are never shown publicly. Contact details are shared only between the customer and pandit of an accepted, confirmed or completed booking, and for pandits only via their chosen contact method.
  • Verification documents are stored outside the public website, visible only to administrators (every view is recorded in an audit log) and automatically deleted after the configured retention period once a decision is made.
  • Pandit profiles show an approximate city area on maps — never a home address.
  • Reviews display the reviewer's first name and last initial only.
  • We do not store card details. When online payments are enabled, they are handled by the payment gateway.

Terms of use

  • Pandit Finder connects customers with independent pandits. Pandits set their own services, prices and availability.
  • A booking request is not a confirmed booking until the pandit accepts it and the customer confirms.
  • Information marked "self-declared" is provided by the pandit and has not been independently verified.
  • Reviews may be written only for completed bookings and may be moderated if they break these terms.
  • Accounts that misuse the platform may be suspended.

Cancellation & rescheduling policy

  • Pending requests can be cancelled by the customer at any time.
  • Accepted or confirmed bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled by the customer up to 24 hours before the ceremony start time. After that, please contact the pandit or support.
  • A rescheduled booking returns to Pending and must be accepted again by the pandit.
  • Pandits may cancel accepted bookings before the ceremony with a reason; the customer is notified immediately.
  • Refunds for online payments on cancelled bookings are processed by an administrator through the payment gateway.
